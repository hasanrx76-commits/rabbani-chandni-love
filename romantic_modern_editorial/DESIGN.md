## Brand & Style
The design system evokes the tactile romance and timeless poise of an haute-couture editorial wedding monograph. Built to celebrate unity, warmth, and refined intimacy, it balances the poised structure of classic editorial typography with tender, organic softness.

The experience feels like paging through a bespoke linen-bound keepsake: spacious, graceful, and deliberately serene. Visual noise is stripped away in favor of generous alabaster breathing room, delicate serif titling, warm sunlit undertones, and brushed metallic accents that guide guests through the celebration with quiet elegance.

## Colors
The palette captures sunlit stone, dried florals, heirloom jewelry, and rich espresso ink:

- **Primary Accent (`#9E3B4D` - Burnt Terracotta Rose):** Used for focal interactive calls-to-action, active indicators, monogram details, and romantic highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C59B4B` - Brushed Champagne Gold):** Reserved for ceremonial emblems, subtle border filigrees, badges, date lockups, and refined micro-details.
- **Tertiary Surface (`#FCEFE9` - Soft Peach Blush):** Serves as card fills, nested highlight panels, modal canvas layers, and interactive hover tints.
- **Neutral Ink (`#241C1D` - Espresso Charcoal):** Deep, warm near-black providing contrast for typography and fine-line iconography without the harshness of pure black.
- **Base Surfaces:** Canvas base rests on `#FAF7F2` (Warm Ivory) with foreground panels elevated on `#FFFDF9` (Delicate Pearl Alabaster).

This system operates exclusively in light mode to retain the luminous, print-inspired atmosphere.

## Typography
The typographic architecture relies on high-contrast pairings: the stately elegance of Playfair Display for headers and emotive statements, paired with the modern clarity and approachability of Plus Jakarta Sans for operational readability and details.

- **Editorial Headings:** Playfair Display introduces rhythm and poise. Display headlines use italics sparingly for poetic emphasis (e.g., vows, couple names, venue highlights).
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures itineraries, registry items, and long-form RSVP options remain effortlessly legible on all screen sizes.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to create the understated authority of luxury invitations.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid system designed around airy margins and graceful whitespace:

- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (32px), `margin-desktop` (64px). Main content caps at a maximum width of 1280px to preserve comfortable reading measures.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter-md` (24px), `margin-tablet` (40px). Multi-column schedules collapse into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (16px), `margin-mobile` (20px). Layout stacks vertically with asymmetrical editorial stagger on photo highlights and itinerary entries.

Vertical pacing prioritizes openness: use `space-3xl` and `space-4xl` between major narrative sections to replicate the relaxed tempo of high-end print publishing.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is suggested subtly through soft, warm ambient diffusion rather than stark digital drop shadows. Surfaces mimic natural light falling upon fine art paper:

- **Surface 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAF7F2` with raw unshadowed presence.
- **Surface 1 (Cards & Modals):** `#FFFDF9` resting on an ambient shadow tinted with warm burnt rose: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(158, 59, 77, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 28, 29, 0.04)`.
- **Surface 2 (Floating Nav & Drawers):** `#FFFDF9` with backdrop blur (`blur(12px)`) at 90% opacity, paired with a deeper glow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(158, 59, 77, 0.10)`.
- **Borders:** Low-contrast hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(197, 155, 75, 0.25)` or `rgba(36, 28, 29, 0.07)`) furnish tactile definition without interrupting the light environment.

## Shapes
Geometry embodies soft curvature and architectural balance. With `roundedness: 2`, structural surfaces incorporate 16px (`1rem`) corner radii for interactive cards and input fields, expanding to 24px (`1.5rem`) for celebration showcase panels and modal sheets.

Buttons, badge tags, and pill tabs retain fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) to provide an organic, hand-held touch that balances the crisp baseline of serif typography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Burnt Terracotta Rose (`#9E3B4D`) background with `#FFFDF9` text. Full pill border radius (`9999px`), 14px uppercase label tracking. Hover state transitions to deep rosewood with an ambient warm glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px champagne gold border (`#C59B4B`), espresso text (`#241C1D`). Hover shifts to a `#FCEFE9` blush tint.
- **Text Action:** Espresso charcoal with an animated hairline underline in brushed gold.

### Cards
- **Event & Itinerary Cards:** Pearl Alabaster (`#FFFDF9`) base, 16px corner radius, hairline warm border (`1px solid rgba(197, 155, 75, 0.2)`), finished with the warm ambient shadow. Card headers pair a gold micro-label for dates/times with Playfair Display for the event title.
- **Highlight / Story Cards:** Soft Peach Blush (`#FCEFE9`) tint with internal padding of 24px (`space-lg`) to spotlight key moments like attire guides or personal notes.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Category Chips:** Pill-shaped, subtle peach fill (`#FCEFE9`) with burnt rose text (`#9E3B4D`) and a 0.08em tracked `label-sm` font.
- **Selected Filter Chip:** Solid Burnt Terracotta Rose (`#9E3B4D`) with white text and delicate inset gold accent dot.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- **Text Fields:** Surface set to `#FFFDF9` with a 1px border (`rgba(36, 28, 29, 0.15)`), 16px border radius, and generous 14px by 18px padding.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#9E3B4D` accompanied by a soft 3px outer halo in `rgba(158, 59, 77, 0.12)`. No harsh browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular radios and rounded 4px checkboxes tinted in Burnt Terracotta Rose (`#9E3B4D`) with smooth interior scale animations upon selection.

### Dedicated Wedding Modules
- **Countdown Lockup:** Four soft-peach columns showcasing large Playfair Display numerals with brushed gold sub-labels (`DAYS`, `HOURS`, `MINS`, `SECS`).
- **RSVP Accordion:** Clean, uncluttered drawer panels segmented by hairline borders in brushed champagne gold.
- **Audio / Celebration Guestbook Player:** Floating frosted glass pill (`#FFFDF9` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) elevated with warm terracotta controls.
